Refund Policy
Last Updated: 6 October 2026
This policy explains how JD Hardware processes refunds for eligible purchases.
1. Refund Eligibility
Refunds may be available for:
* Eligible non-defective returns
* Defective products
* Damaged products
* Incorrectly supplied products
* Cancelled orders
* Other circumstances requiring a refund under UK consumer law
2. Standard Refund Processing Time
We aim to process eligible refunds within 10 days after the returned product has been received and approved.
This is our standard processing target and does not override statutory refund deadlines.
3. Statutory Cancellation Refunds
Where a customer exercises the statutory right to cancel a distance purchase, we will refund the required amounts within 14 days of receiving the returned goods or receiving evidence that they have been sent back, whichever is earlier.
Where goods have not yet been delivered, applicable statutory refund deadlines will be followed.
4. Refund Payment Method
Refunds are normally issued to the original payment method unless another method is expressly agreed where legally permitted.
We do not charge a refund processing fee.
5. Return Shipping Fees
Approved returns qualify for free return shipping.
Return Shipping Fee: £0.00
6. Restocking Fees
We do not charge restocking fees.
Restocking Fee: £0.00
7. Original Delivery Charges
Our standard UK delivery is free.
Where an alternative paid delivery service has been offered, statutory cancellation refunds will include the cost of standard delivery where required by law, but may exclude additional charges for an upgraded delivery service.
8. Damaged or Defective Products
Where a product is faulty, damaged or not as described, we will provide the appropriate remedy under applicable consumer law.
9. Partial Refunds
Where permitted by law, a reduction may apply if a returned non-defective product has lost value due to handling beyond what was necessary to inspect it.
Any such reduction will be explained.
10. Refunds for Promotional Purchases
Refunds generally reflect the amount actually paid, subject to applicable statutory requirements.
11. Delayed Refunds
If your refund has not appeared after processing:
* Check your original payment account.
* Contact your bank or payment provider.
* Email our support team with your order number.
12. Duplicate Payments
If you believe you have been charged twice, contact us promptly so the transaction can be reviewed.
